Interactive preflop RFI and defense ranges by position

Step through your position, then pick a villain to see the defense range. Pick None to see your RFI strategy from that seat.

How to use

  • Position: step with the ◀ ▶ arrows, the arrow keys, or swipe on mobile.
  • RFI: select None to see your raise-first-in range from the current seat.
  • Defense: select any earlier position to see your 3-bet and calling range against their open.
Based on standard preflop charts for 100BB cash. Treat as a default — adjust to opponents, stack depth, and game dynamics.
